# Course Platform

Students buy courses, watch lessons, and can ask for their money back within a short window.

## Language

### Buying

**Purchase**:
A student's (or team buyer's) payment for a course, covering one or more seats.
_Avoid_: Order, transaction

**Amount Paid**:
What the buyer was actually charged on a Purchase, after any PPP discount and across all seats.
_Avoid_: Price (that is the course's list price)

**Team Seat**:
One place on a multi-seat Purchase, handed to another student by redeeming a code. The Purchase stays the buyer's.
_Avoid_: Coupon (when talking about the seat itself)

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A buyer's ask to have a Purchase refunded, in their own words. It is Requested, then Approved or Declined by an admin; it cannot be withdrawn. A Purchase gets at most one Refund Request that is Approved or Declined.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund**:
The money returned once a Refund Request is Approved: always the full Amount Paid, never partial. It ends access to the course.
_Avoid_: Reversal, partial refund

**Declined**:
The outcome of a Refund Request that will not be refunded. No Refund exists for it.
_Avoid_: Denied, rejected

**Refund Window**:
The day of a Purchase plus the 14 calendar days after it, during which a Refund Request may be raised.
_Avoid_: Cancel window

**Refundable**:
A Purchase is Refundable while it is inside its Refund Window, under 2 hours have been watched, under half the lessons are completed, it has never had a Refund Request, and none of its Team Seats has been redeemed. Holders of a redeemed Team Seat never have a Refundable Purchase.

**Net Revenue**:
The total Amount Paid on a course's Purchases, minus their Refunds.
_Avoid_: Revenue (unqualified)

**Chargeback**:
A dispute raised by the buyer's bank, outside this platform. Not the same thing as a Refund Request.
